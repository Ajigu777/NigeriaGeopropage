GEODETIC PROBLEM STATEMENT

Bridging Nigeria's 150-Metre Coordinate Gap

Official land registries and modern satellite positioning speak two conflicting languages. NigeriaGeo Pro brings them into mathematical harmony.

Two Parallel Address Systems for the Same Spot

In Nigeria, cadastral survey beacons, Certificates of Occupancy (C of O), and state land boundary plans are registered under the colonial-origin Minna Datum (Clarke 1880 ellipsoid). However, every handheld GPS rover, drone telemetry unit, smartphone, and satellite layer operates on WGS84.

Because these two ellipsoidal references differ in physical center and orientation, coordinates representing the exact same survey beacon disagree by between 100 and 200 metres on the ground. NigeriaGeo Pro implements the standardized EPSG:1061 Molodensky transformation parameters combined with Transverse Mercator projections to convert between them flawlessly.
